How does Sub of the Day work?

One sandwich, one price, one day, with two upgrades that keep the meal under $8.

  1. Pick the day sandwich: 6-inch for $4.99.
  2. Make it a meal for $6.99: add chips or 2 cookies and a 20oz fountain drink, which is $2 more.
  3. Upgrade to a footlong for $7.99: add $3 to the $4.99 6-inch price.
  4. Check your store in the app first: participating stores only, prices higher in California, Washington, Alaska and Hawaii, plus tax, and it cannot be combined with other offers.

Sub of the Day is not a code deal and it is not a footlong deal at $4.99. The $4.99 price is for the 6-inch day sandwich. Meal of the Day at $6.99 is the same day sandwich as a meal, not a different sandwich.

How do you check today Sub of the Day?

Day of the week tells you the sandwich, the app tells you if your store takes part.

  1. Check the day: Monday Meatball, Tuesday Tuna, Wednesday Sweet Onion, Thursday Turkey, Friday Ham, Saturday B.M.T., Sunday Spicy Italian.
  2. Open the Subway app, pick your store, and look for the Sub of the Day section.
  3. If your store does not show it, that store does not take part that day, and the regular price applies.

Frequently asked questions about Subway Sub of the Day

What is Subway Sub of the Day?

Sub of the Day is a different 6-inch sandwich every day of the week for $4.99 at participating US Subway stores. Monday is Meatball Marinara, Tuesday is Classic Tuna, Wednesday is Sweet Onion Chicken Teriyaki, Thursday is Oven-Roasted Turkey, Friday is Black Forest Ham, Saturday is Italian B.M.T., and Sunday is Spicy Italian.

How much is Sub of the Day?

$4.99 for a 6-inch sandwich. Make it a meal for $6.99, which adds chips or 2 cookies and a 20oz fountain drink. Upgrade to a footlong for $3 more, which is $7.99. Prices are higher in California, Washington, Alaska and Hawaii, plus tax.

Which Sub of the Day saves the most money?

Wednesday, Sweet Onion Chicken Teriyaki, saves $3.07 against its 19-store regular price of $8.06. Saturday Italian B.M.T. is next at $2.71 saved, then Tuesday Classic Tuna at $2.36.

Which Sub of the Day is lowest in calories?

Wednesday, Sweet Onion Chicken Teriyaki, at 430 calories with 29g protein, the highest protein of the 7 days. Thursday Oven-Roasted Turkey is next at 480 calories.

Which Sub of the Day is lowest in sodium?

Tuesday, Classic Tuna, at 950mg sodium with 27g protein and 570 calories. Sunday Spicy Italian is highest at 1,690mg sodium.

Do I need a code for Sub of the Day?

No code is needed. Ask for the day sandwich, or pick it in the app, and the $4.99 price applies at participating stores. It cannot be combined with other offers.

Is Sub of the Day available at every Subway?

No, at participating US stores only. A minority of franchise stores do not take part, and prices vary by store, so check your store in the app before you go.
