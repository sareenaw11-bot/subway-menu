Which Subway sandwiches are cheapest?

Start with the two $4.37 sandwiches, then the next tier. The cheapest sandwich is not always the best value, because protein differs at the same price.

Cold Cut Combo at $4.37 gives 25g of protein for 530 calories, while B.L.T. at the same $4.37 gives 18g of protein for 480 calories and 800mg of sodium. Veggie Delite at $6.32 is next, with 320 calories and 600mg of sodium. Pizza Sub at $6.50, priced at 13 of 19 stores, and Meatball Marinara at $6.95 follow. Spicy Italian at $6.96 and Black Forest Ham at $6.97 sit just under $7.00.

Which Subway sandwich has the most protein?

5 Meat Italian leads, then a clear second group at 35g. The 35g group is the smarter protein pick for most readers, because its sodium is lower.

5 Meat Italian gives 40g of protein, 680 calories and 1,940mg of sodium for $9.42. Chicken and Bacon Ranch gives 35g, 580 calories and 1,230mg of sodium for $8.54. The four Fresh Fit avocado sandwiches also give 35g each: Grilled Chicken and Fresh Avocado at 450 calories and 800mg of sodium, Grilled Chicken and Smashed Avocado at 470 calories and 930mg, Seasoned Steak and Fresh Avocado at 430 calories and 1,040mg, and Seasoned Steak and Smashed Avocado at 460 calories and 1,170mg.

Which Subway sandwich is lowest in calories and sodium?

Lowest calories and lowest sodium are two different sandwiches, so check both numbers before you pick.

Ham and Turkey Stacker is lowest in calories at 290, with 20g of protein and 1,000mg of sodium. Veggie Delite is second lowest in calories at 320, and lowest in sodium at 600mg, with 17g of protein. Turkey and Ranch Delite is third lowest in calories at 380, with 26g of protein and 1,140mg of sodium. Grilled Chicken at 510 calories and 830mg of sodium, and Grilled Chicken and Fresh Avocado at 450 calories and 800mg of sodium, are the low-sodium meat options. Big Hot Pastrami is highest in sodium at 2,070mg, and its price is unavailable in the sample.

What does a 6-inch sandwich cost as a footlong?

This page does not list footlong prices, because the sample used here covers 6-inch sandwiches only.

Do not double the 6-inch price and treat it as a footlong price. Franchise stores set footlong prices separately, and the ratio varies by store and by item. Check the Subway app or your store for the footlong price before you order, and use the 6-inch table here to compare sandwiches, not to price a footlong.

How do you choose a Subway sandwich?

Use this order, so one number does not decide the whole order for you.

  1. Pick the goal first: cheapest, most protein, lowest calories, or lowest sodium.
  2. Pick the sandwich from the goal table above, not from a taste ranking with no numbers.
  3. Check sodium as well as protein. A 40g sandwich at 1,940mg of sodium is a different order from a 35g sandwich at 800mg.
  4. Remember bread, cheese, sauce and extras change calories and sodium. The table is the standard build.
  5. Check the price at your store, because the table price is a 19-store average, not a fixed national price.
  6. For a different format, use the tool to filter sandwiches, or see wraps, salads and bowls on this site.

Frequently asked questions about Subway sandwiches

Which Subway sandwich is cheapest?

Cold Cut Combo and B.L.T., both $4.37 for a 6-inch in the 19-store sample. Veggie Delite is next at $6.32, and it is also the lowest-sodium sandwich on this page.

Which Subway sandwich has the most protein?

5 Meat Italian, with 40g of protein in a 6-inch. It also has 680 calories and 1,940mg of sodium, so it is a protein pick, not a low-sodium pick. The Fresh Fit chicken and steak avocado sandwiches give 35g with less sodium.

Which Subway sandwich is lowest in calories?

Ham and Turkey Stacker, at 290 calories for a 6-inch, followed by Veggie Delite at 320 calories. Both are 6-inch figures, and bread, cheese, sauce and extras change the final total.

Which Subway sandwich is healthiest?

It depends on the goal. For lowest calories and sodium, Veggie Delite leads at 320 calories and 600mg of sodium. For protein with moderate sodium, Grilled Chicken and Fresh Avocado gives 35g of protein, 450 calories and 800mg of sodium.

How much does a Subway footlong sandwich cost?

Footlong prices are not in the 19-store sample used here, which covers 6-inch sandwiches only, so this page does not list a footlong table. Check the Subway app or your store for the footlong price, because franchise stores set their own prices.

Why do Subway sandwich prices vary by store?

Subway stores are franchises, and each store sets its own prices. The prices on this page are averages from 19 sampled stores, not a national fixed price, so your store may charge more or less.

How does this page check Subway sandwiches?

Prices and nutrition come from two separate sources, and the page keeps them separate.

Prices are averages from 19 real Subway stores, sampled in 11 states plus DC, as supplied on October 25, 2026. Most priced sandwiches are priced at all 19 stores. Pizza Sub is priced at 13 stores, Veggie Patty at 17 stores, and four sandwiches have no price in the sample, so they show Price unavailable.
