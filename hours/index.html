Subway Hours: When Is Subway Open, and When Is It Closed?

See 2026 Holiday Hours ↓

Most US Subway stores run 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM Monday through Saturday, and 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM on Sunday. Most stores close only on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. Check your own store, because Subway stores are franchises and each store sets its own hours.

Typical means most US stores, not your store. Closed at most stores means most, not all, with airport, hospital and travel plaza exceptions named in the table. No exact holiday closing time is invented where franchise stores decide it themselves.

Is Subway open on Christmas Day?

Most US Subway stores are closed on Christmas Day, Friday December 25, 2026, with airport, hospital and travel plaza locations as the main exceptions.

Christmas Day is one of only two days when most Subway stores close. Christmas Eve, Thursday December 24, 2026, is open with reduced hours, and many stores close early around 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Stores reopen with normal hours on Saturday December 26, 2026. If you need food on Christmas Day, check an airport, hospital or travel plaza store first, and do not assume a neighborhood store is open.

Is Subway open on Thanksgiving Day?

Most US Subway stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday November 26, 2026, with highway, airport, hospital and 24-hour locations as the main exceptions.

Thanksgiving Day is the other chain-wide closure, along with Christmas Day. Black Friday, Friday November 27, 2026, is open with normal Friday hours at most stores, except mall stores, which follow mall hours. Stock up the day before Thanksgiving, because the day itself has the fewest open stores of any US holiday except Christmas Day.

Is Subway open on Christmas Eve and New Year Eve?

Both are open days with reduced hours, not closed days, and no single closing time fits all stores.

Christmas Eve: open, reduced, many stores close early around 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM, travel-area stores stay open later, suburban stores close early. New Year Eve, Thursday December 31, 2026: open, normal or reduced, some stores close early, party-area stores may stay open later. New Year Day, Thursday January 1, 2026, and Friday January 1, 2027: open, reduced, late opening around 10:00 AM is common, and a few stores stay closed all day.

Is Subway open on Easter, July 4, Memorial Day and Labor Day?

Yes, at most stores, with reduced hours on Easter and on July 4 evening in some tourist areas.

Easter Sunday, April 5, 2026: mostly open, reduced hours, mall and roadside stores more likely open, neighborhood stores often shorten hours, some franchise stores close fully. Memorial Day, May 25, 2026, and Labor Day, September 7, 2026: open, normal hours at most stores. Independence Day, Saturday July 4, 2026: open, normal or reduced hours, tourist-area stores may stay open later, small-town stores sometimes close early. Observed holiday note: July 4, 2026 is a Saturday, some offices observe Friday July 3, but Subway stores generally stay open on both days.

Which holidays are federal, and which are cultural days?

Federal holidays and cultural days do not follow the same hours rule.

Federal holidays: New Year Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day. Cultural days that are not US federal Subway closures: Valentine Day, St. Patrick Day, Mardi Gras, Cinco de Mayo, Mother Day, Father Day, Halloween, Easter Monday, Boxing Day. Boxing Day, December 26, is a UK and Canada holiday, not a US federal holiday, and US stores reopen with normal Saturday hours on December 26, 2026. Good Friday, April 3, 2026, has no standard rule at Subway, so some stores close and some open as usual, call your store.

How do you check your own Subway store hours?

Use this order. Calling is the most reliable check on a holiday.

  1. Use the official Subway store locator, enter your ZIP code or city, and open your store page for its current weekly and holiday hours.
  2. Check the Subway app, which shows whether your store is currently open and accepting mobile orders.
  3. Check Google Maps for the same store, and compare it with the locator, because Maps hours can be crowdsourced and out of date.
  4. Call the store directly, especially on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year Eve and New Year Day.
  5. If the store is closed, check the next store, an airport or travel plaza store, or delivery, before you travel.

Why do Subway hours vary so much?

Because Subway is a franchise system, not a company-owned chain with one schedule.

Each franchise owner sets hours based on foot traffic, rent, staffing, host store hours, and local demand. Urban stores open earlier for commuters, sometimes at 6:00 AM. Suburban stores lean to 7:00 AM or 8:00 AM. Rural stores may start at 9:00 AM or 10:00 AM. One US store runs 7:30 AM to 12:00 AM daily, another runs 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily, another runs 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM with Sunday 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM. The same pattern shows up in the UK, where 1,808 stores have 1,144 different weekly schedules, and the most common single schedule covers only 2.4% of stores.

What do you do if your Subway store is closed?

Do not travel to a second store without checking it first, because holiday closures cluster.

Check an airport, hospital or travel plaza store, which are likeliest to stay open on Thanksgiving and Christmas Day. Check delivery in the app, because delivery availability can differ from walk-in hours. For Thanksgiving and Christmas Day, stock up the day before, because those are the two days with the fewest open stores. For Christmas Eve and New Year Eve, go earlier in the day, because early closing is the most common reduced-hours pattern.
