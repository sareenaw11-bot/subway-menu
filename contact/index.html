Contact Us

Use the email below for corrections, questions about a price or nutrition figure, or a request about your personal data under the Privacy Policy.

Email: contact@subwaymenuss.us

What to include in your message: the page URL, the item or date you are asking about, and what you found at your store, if you are reporting a price or hours difference. A store name and city help, a photo is not required.

What this site does not do by email: this site does not take orders, it does not run a Subway store, and it cannot change a price or a deal at your local store. For an order problem, contact your store or Subway directly.

Response time is not guaranteed, because this is a small independent site, not a Subway customer service team.
